Working

How the club works, day to day: the monthly theme, hearts, long-term series, events and the exhibition, and how we give feedback.

What the club does

Monthly themesA new theme every month. Share one photo that fits.

Every month a new theme goes up on the home page. Share one photo that fits before the month ends; interpret it however you like, and tick This is my entry for the monthly theme when you send it.

The gallery collects every entry, and the theme archive keeps each month's theme with its entries and the ones members gave the most hearts.

HeartsGive a heart to any photo you like.

Open any photo, new or old, and tap the heart beside its title. One heart per photo, on as many photos as you like (up to 20 a day). Tapped by mistake? Tap again to take it back, on the same day; after that the heart stays. You can't give your own photos a heart.

Each photo shows how many hearts it has, as they come in; who gave which heart stays secret. When a month ends, the photos that got the most hearts during that month become the Photos of the month, on the home page and in the theme archive.

DownloadsPosters, cheat sheets and guides from the club.

The admins share event posters, cheat sheets, guides and forms on the home page and the Downloads page, to print, forward or keep on your phone. Some are for everyone; others only show when you're signed in as a member.

Photographer pagesEvery member who shares photos has a page.

Each page shows all of that member's photos, linked from their name under each photo and from the Photographers page. Only members see these pages.

Add a picture of yourself and a line about you on the Share a photo page, if you like: where you live, what you photograph, how long you've been in Germany. Your picture shows as a small round icon next to your name.

EventsPhoto walks, workshops, exhibitions and meet-ups.

They're all on the Events page. Members tap I'm coming, so the organisers know who to expect, and see who else is going. Anyone can add an event to their calendar or share it on WhatsApp.

The exhibitionA public selection, only with the photographer's OK.

The Exhibition page shows a selection of members' photos to everyone, not only members. The club's admins choose them, but only from photos whose photographers have agreed: tick the box when you share a photo if you're happy for it to be shown there.

Changed your mind? Tell an admin and the photo comes off the Exhibition page straight away.

Long-term series

Alongside the monthly themes, the club keeps series that grow over months and years. When you share a photo, choose a series if it fits. Members find each series on the home page and in the gallery.

Giving feedback

Every comment here is written for the person who took the photo. Before you post, read it back as if it were about your own picture.

Feedback welcomeSuggestions are wanted. Start with what works, then your suggestion.
Just sharingNo critique needed. Say what you enjoy and leave out suggestions.

You choose one of these when you share a photo, and the photo shows which. The gallery's Feedback welcome filter shows the photos where suggestions are wanted.

  • Start with what works. Name something you genuinely like, then make your suggestion.
  • Be specific. Say what you'd change and why.
  • Talk about the photo, not the person. No comments on anyone's skill, gear or age.
  • Leave room for taste. “I would try…” works better than “You should…”.
Comments, deleting and reporting

Members can comment on any photo in the gallery, under the photo when you open it. Like the photos, comments are only for members, and your name is shown with each one. You can delete your own comments at any time.

If a comment breaks the rules above, tap Report under it: the admins will look at it, and the person who wrote it won't know who reported it.

Your photos

Your photos stay yoursYou keep full copyright. The gallery is private.

Posting here only gives the club permission to show your photo to its members, and, if you tick the box for it, in the public exhibition. The gallery itself is private: people need an invite code to see it, and it isn't listed by search engines.

Only share photos you took yourself, and never repost or edit another member's photo without asking them first. More in the privacy policy.

What you can postAny genre, from landscapes to phone snapshots.

Landscapes, street, macro, abstract and phone snapshots are all welcome. Mark AI-generated or composited images as such. People who can be clearly identified should have agreed to be photographed.
