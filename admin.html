                      Backup

                      Download everything in the club as one .zip file: the gallery's photos with their titles and details, members and their invite codes, comments and votes. Keep it somewhere private, because it contains the invite codes.

                      Once a month is plenty. Once the gallery has hundreds of photos, do it on a computer: the file gets large.

                      Restore from a backup

                      Brings back photos and members that are missing, with their comments and votes, from a backup file. Nothing already in the club is changed or deleted, so it's safe to try.
