About the club

The Conscious Frame is a place to show your photos and get better at taking them. Here's how it works and the few rules that keep it friendly.

Weekly themes and contests

Every Monday a new theme goes up on the home page. Share one photo that fits by Sunday evening; interpret it however you like.

Each member has three votes a week for photos added to the gallery that week. Open a photo and tap Vote; tap it again to take the vote back. Votes stay secret until the week is over. On Monday, the three photos with the most votes become Photos of the week on the home page. You can't vote for your own photos.

Alongside that, a bigger contest runs each month: about three weeks of entries, then a week of voting. Winners are shown to members on the home page and in the gallery.

Giving feedback

Members can comment on any photo in the gallery, under the photo when you open it. Like the photos, comments are only for members, and your name is shown with each one. You can delete your own comments at any time.

Every comment here is written for the person who took the photo. Before you post, read it back as if it were about your own picture.

  • Start with what works. Name something you genuinely like, then make your suggestion.
  • Be specific. Say what you'd change and why.
  • Talk about the photo, not the person. No comments on anyone's skill, gear or age.
  • Leave room for taste. “I would try…” works better than “You should…”.

Your photos stay yours

You keep full copyright of everything you share. Posting here only gives the club permission to show your photo to its members. The gallery is private: people need an invite code to see it, and it isn't listed by search engines. Only share photos you took yourself, and never repost or edit another member's photo without asking them first.

What you can post

Any genre is welcome, from landscapes and street to macro, abstract and phone snapshots. Mark AI-generated or composited images as such. People who can be clearly identified should have agreed to be photographed.

Joining

Membership is free. Send us a short note with your name and, if you like, a link to a few of your photos. We'll reply with your personal invite code.

With your code you can see the members' gallery, comment and vote, and send photos from your phone on the Share a photo page. A club admin checks each photo before it appears in the gallery, and you can see on that page whether it's been approved.

Questions

Who can see my photos?

Only club members, signed in with their invite code. Visitors to the site see the weekly theme and contests, but no photos.

Do I need a proper camera?

No. Plenty of members shoot mostly on their phones.

How many photos can I share?

Up to five a week. Fewer, better photos tend to get more thoughtful comments.

Someone left a comment that upset me. What can I do?

Email the club. Admins can remove any comment, and we read every message and reply within a day or two.
