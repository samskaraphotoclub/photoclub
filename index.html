How we give feedback

Start with what works

Every comment begins with something you genuinely like about the photo. Then the suggestion.

Be specific

“Nice shot” is pleasant. “The bright patch on the left pulls my eye away” is something the photographer can use.

Ask before you edit

Photos belong to the people who took them. Never repost or rework someone else's picture without asking.
