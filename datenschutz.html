Privacy policy

Datenschutzerklärung: what personal data this website handles, why, and your rights. Last updated October 2026.

In short

  • No cookies, no tracking, no advertising. The site has no analytics and loads no fonts or scripts from other companies.
  • Visitors: GitHub, which hosts the pages, records your IP address for security when you open a page.
  • Members: the club stores what you share (your name, photos, comments and votes) so the members' gallery works. Only members and the club's admins can see it.
  • The exhibition: a photo appears on the public Exhibition page only if its photographer agreed.

Visiting the website

The pages are hosted by GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you open a page, your browser sends GitHub your IP address and technical details such as the browser you use, the time and the page you asked for. GitHub records visitors' IP addresses for security, whether or not they have a GitHub account. The club itself doesn't see or keep these records.

Legal basis: our legitimate interest in showing the website safely and reliably (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). GitHub is certified under the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework, so transfers to it in the USA are covered by the European Commission's adequacy decision (Art. 45 GDPR). More in GitHub's privacy statement.

The members' gallery

The members' gallery, the photos members send, comments and votes, and the files on the Downloads page are kept in the club's own small program on Cloudflare (Cloudflare Workers and Workers KV), a service of Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. Cloudflare stores this data for the club as a processor under its data processing agreement (Art. 28 GDPR), in data centres in several countries, including the USA. Cloudflare is certified under the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework (Art. 45 GDPR). When your browser loads photos, downloads or the gallery, Cloudflare handles your IP address to deliver them and to protect its network. The club doesn't keep a record of visits.

What is stored about members

  • Your name and invite code, entered by an admin when you're invited, and the date.
  • Photos you share, with the title, description, category, series and camera settings in the form. Your phone resizes each photo before sending it, which removes the location and other hidden details from the file.
  • Your last 30 photos sent, and what the admins decided, with any note they wrote to you.
  • Your comments, shown to members with your name.
  • A line about yourself, if you write one, shown to members on your photographer page. Delete it there at any time.
  • Events you sign up for. Members and admins see your name in the list of who's coming; visitors don't. Tap the button again to take it back.
  • Your votes. Members see how many votes each photo has, but no one sees who voted for what. Your votes are deleted automatically after 120 days; a photo's total stays with the photo.
  • Comments you report to the admins, with your name and the reason you give. The admins see this; the person who wrote the comment doesn't. A report is deleted when an admin deals with it.
  • The theme archive: each week's theme and the photos with the most votes, with their vote counts.
  • Counters of how many photos, comments and reports you've sent recently, so the club's limits work. The daily counters are deleted after two days.

Who sees it

Members see the photos in the gallery, with the photographer's name, and members' comments with their names. The club's admins see everything listed above, including invite codes, so they can run the club. We don't sell your data, use it for advertising, or pass it to anyone else.

Why, and on what legal basis

To run the members' gallery you joined, which is the service you asked for (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR), and, for the limits and reports that keep it fair and friendly, our legitimate interest in preventing misuse (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

How long it's kept

Photos and comments stay until you or an admin delete them. You can delete your own comments at any time, and the admins delete photos on request. When you leave the club, your invite code is deleted; tell us if your photos and comments should go too. About once a month an admin saves a backup of the gallery and its records on their own device. We keep backups for at most 12 months, so deleted photos and comments disappear from them within a year.

The public exhibition

The Exhibition page shows a selection of members' photos to everyone, with the photographer's name. A photo is only shown there if its photographer agreed, for example by ticking the box when sharing it. You can withdraw your agreement at any time (Art. 7(3) GDPR): tell an admin, and the photo comes off the Exhibition page straight away. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).

People in photos

Members may only share photos in which people can be recognised if those people have agreed to it. If you're in a photo on this site and want it removed, write to us and we'll take it down.

What the site keeps on your device

The site sets no cookies. It uses your browser's own storage (local storage) for a few things you ask for: your invite code, so you stay signed in until you tap Sign out; whether you chose light or dark mode; and, on admins' devices, the admin page's settings. None of this is used to follow you, and it never leaves your device except to sign you in to the club. As it's needed for the service you're using, no consent is required (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG).

Calendar and WhatsApp buttons

Add to calendar makes the calendar file on your own device; nothing is sent anywhere. The Google Calendar and Share on WhatsApp buttons open those services with the event's or theme's details, only when you tap them. From then on, Google's or WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies.

Writing to us

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply, and delete them once the matter is settled, unless the law requires us to keep them (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR).

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • get a copy of the data we hold about you (Art. 15 GDPR)
  • have it corrected (Art. 16) or deleted (Art. 17)
  • have its use restricted (Art. 18)
  • receive it in a common format, to take elsewhere (Art. 20)
  • object to uses based on our legitimate interests (Art. 21)
  • withdraw your consent at any time, with effect for the future (Art. 7(3))

Write to samskara.germany@gmail.com. You can also complain to a data protection authority (Art. 77 GDPR), for example the one for the federal state where the association is based: name of the state's data protection authority.

We don't make automated decisions about anyone, and we don't build profiles.
